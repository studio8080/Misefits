How it works

  1. Load a plan or start blank. Open the app and choose “From a plan” to load a PDF or image (the first page of a PDF is used). No plan yet? Choose “From scratch” and enter the room size in mm — rectangle, L-shape or U-shape.
  2. Set the scale. Click “Set scale by clicking 2 points”, click both ends of a dimension you know (a wall, a door, a dimension line) and type the real length in mm. From then on, everything on the sheet is measured at real size.
  3. Place fixtures. Pick a category in the side panel and click a part to add it, or drag it onto the canvas. Tap a fixture to change its width, depth or angle. Parts snap to walls and to each other with pink guide lines.
  4. Check aisles and seats. Press M (or 📐 Measure) and click two points. The Summary panel shows seats, fixture area, floor area and coverage.
  5. Save and export. Save the project as JSON to keep editing later, or export PNG/PDF. The grid is never printed.
Tip: calibrate on the longest dimension you can find. A small clicking error on a short door width becomes a big error across the whole room.

FAQ

What happens if I clear browser data or lose my key?

Clearing site data removes locally saved plans and Pro activation. Keep a JSON backup and your license email. Entering your key again uses a new activation slot; if you reach the five-slot limit, contact our support form with the email address used for purchase. Private browsing may not retain your work after the session ends. Do not publish your license key or payment confirmation URL.

Can I use Pro in both Chrome and Edge?

Yes. Enter the same MiseFits license key in each browser. Chrome and Edge on the same computer use two of your five activation slots. Plans and layouts do not sync automatically between browsers: save a JSON file and open it in the other browser. MiseFits and MenuFits have separate license keys.

Which browser and device should I use?

For placing fixtures and checking dimensions, we recommend the latest Chrome or Edge on a computer with a mouse or trackpad. An internet connection is required to load the drawing libraries. On Safari, Firefox, phones or tablets, try loading, placing and exporting with the free version before buying Pro.

If saving or exporting does not work inside a social or other app, open this site directly in Chrome or Edge. Auto-saved work is separate on each device and browser; save a JSON file as a backup. For actual-size printing, check the paper size and scale specified by the export.

Is MiseFits free?

Yes. Loading plans, setting the scale, placing fixtures, measuring aisles, seat counts and PNG/PDF export are free, with no sign-up and no watermark. MiseFits Pro is an optional one-time purchase that adds extra tools.

Do I need to create an account?

No. MiseFits runs in your browser. Your work is auto-saved on your own device, and you can save a project file (JSON) to continue later or on another computer.

Is my floor plan uploaded anywhere?

No. Plans and layouts are processed only inside your browser and are never sent to our servers. See the privacy policy.

Can I see areas in square feet?

Yes. In the English version every area is shown in both m² and sq ft, so you can compare a layout with a listing quoted per square foot. Lengths are entered and shown in millimetres.

Does it tell me whether my layout meets building or fire codes?

No. Seat counts and aisle colours are planning estimates. Accessibility, egress and fire requirements depend on your country, city and type of business — always confirm the final layout with a local architect, certifier or authority.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. MiseFits Pro is a one-time payment of US$19. Your license key is shown after checkout and emailed to you; one key works on up to five devices (counted per browser).

Is MiseFits accessible?

We are working towards WCAG 2.2 AA. You can zoom the page, add parts from the library with Tab and Enter, move a selected part with the arrow keys (10 mm, or 100 mm with Shift), select parts in turn with N, rotate with R and undo with Ctrl+Z. Known limitations: calibrating the scale, measuring and tracing outlines still need a mouse or touch. Seat counts and areas are shown as text in the Summary panel. If something doesn't work for you, use our support form and we will help.
